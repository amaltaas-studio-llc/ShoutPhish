ShoutPhish

Installed. One step before it starts.

ShoutPhish has not read anything yet. Below is what it does with your mail; if that is fine with you, choose Start checking my mail. From then on, each message you open in Gmail is checked as it loads, and a small badge appears beside the sender with what was found.

✓ Low Risk · 4/100 ! Caution · 31/100 ⚠ Suspicious · 58/100 ⛔ High Risk · 82/100 ? Not checked

Click any badge to see the score broken down finding by finding, each with the evidence it came from. Every point is explained; nothing is a black box.

The dashed Not checked badge is the important one. It means ShoutPhish could not read part of the message (usually because Gmail changed its page structure), so it produced no score at all rather than a reassuring one it had not earned.

What it does with your mail

  • Reads the message you have open, checks it, and discards it. Nothing is written to storage and no message is ever sent anywhere by default.
  • Never opens links, loads images, downloads attachments, or looks up a domain. Every check is textual.
  • Never blocks, deletes, or replies. It is advisory: it tells you what it noticed and leaves the decision to you.

Two permissions, and why

mail.google.com Read the open message in order to check it. This is the only site it runs on.
storage Remember your settings. No message content is stored.

Start checking your mail

When you choose Start, ShoutPhish reads each Gmail message you open: who sent it, where replies go and who it was sent to, the subject, the text, the links, the names of attachments, who sent the earlier messages in the conversation, and Gmail's own details about how it was sent. If you later turn on inbox marks, it also reads the sender shown on each row of your inbox. It checks them inside this browser for signs of phishing, keeps none of it, and sends none of it anywhere, unless you choose an AI option further down that says otherwise. You can stop at any time in Settings.

Optional: add an AI reading

The technical checks (sender domains, links, attachments, wording) always run on this device and need no AI. Wording patterns cover English plus Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Hindi and Hinglish. On top of them, ShoutPhish can ask a language model what it makes of the message's intent. It is off until you choose it.

AI mode

A model can never outvote the checks. It contributes at most 15 of the 100 points, cannot remove a finding, and scores nothing at all unless a technical check already found something. To use a model server you run yourself, see Settings. Google explains Chrome's on-device AI in Manage on-device Generative AI models.
